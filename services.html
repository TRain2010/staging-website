Area-based pricing

You pay for the areas you stage

No packages and no per-square-foot guesswork. Each area carries its own price, and your quote is the sum of the ones we agree on at the walkthrough.

Ranges cover a typical home; where a room lands depends on its size, how much furniture it needs, and the price band the house is competing in. Every figure includes design, delivery, installation, a 45-day rental period, and collection after closing.

The process

From first call to collection

Most projects run from consultation to photography in under two weeks.

  1. Step 01

    Walkthrough

    We walk the property with you, talk through the target buyer and the timeline, and say honestly which areas are worth staging and which are not.

  2. Step 02

    Area quote

    You get the list of areas, what each one includes, and a fixed total. Nothing is ordered until you approve it.

  3. Step 03

    Install

    Our crew delivers and places everything in one visit. Most homes are finished inside a day; large estates take two.

  4. Step 04

    Final styling

    The details that photograph: sightlines, layered lighting, fresh greenery, and a last pass through every frame the camera will see.

  5. Step 05

    Collection

    Once the sale closes we collect everything within 48 hours and leave the property exactly as we found it.

Always included
