Questions

Asked often enough to answer here

How much does staging cost?

We price by area, so you pay for the rooms you stage rather than a package. An entry starts at $200, a living area runs $1,500–$2,200, a primary suite with its bath $950–$1,400. A whole house across all sixteen areas lands between $10,400 and $15,700; a smaller listing staged in four or five areas is nearer $4,000. The full schedule lists every area and what each one includes.

How quickly can you stage a property?

Most homes are installed within 72 hours of you approving the plan, and the install itself takes one day. Large estates take two. We keep a few rush slots open each week for listings on a hard deadline.

Do I have to move out?

No. Occupied staging is built for people still living in the house — we work with what you own, add pieces from our inventory, and reset the rooms before each open house.

Is staging really worth it?

Staged homes photograph better, show better, and tend to go under contract sooner than comparable empty or lived-in listings. Staging almost always costs less than the first price reduction it prevents.

What happens to the furniture after the sale?

We collect everything within 48 hours of closing and leave the property clean. If the buyer wants to keep specific pieces, we can usually arrange a purchase.

Do you serve my area?

We cover Seattle, the north end, and the Eastside — Bellevue, Clyde Hill, Medina, Kirkland, Redmond, Sammamish, and Issaquah. Send the address and we'll confirm; anything further out is quoted with travel included.
