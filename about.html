Shoreline · Living room

Our story

Started in a garage. Now twelve thousand square feet.

Top Staging And Design began in 2015 when our founder, an interior designer, kept watching good houses sit on the market because nobody could picture living in them.

The first job was one condo, one van, and furniture borrowed from her own home. Ten years on we run our own warehouse, our own install crews, and a catalogue of pieces chosen for how they photograph and how they scale.

Nothing is subcontracted, which is why we can quote a fixed price and hold a date. We work with listing agents, private sellers, developers, and estate attorneys across Seattle and the Eastside.

How we work

Three things we won't compromise on

01

Dates we can hold

Because the inventory is ours, an install date is a commitment, not a hope. Most homes are staged within 72 hours of the plan being signed off.

02

Prices before we start

One written quote covering design, delivery, install, the rental period, and removal. No hourly creep, no surprise line items at the end.

03

Design that fits the buyer

A first-home condo and a five-bedroom estate need different rooms, not the same room in different sizes. Every plan starts from the comparables.
